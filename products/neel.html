🧪 Commercial Applications

White cotton sheet finishing, chef uniforms, and tablecloths. Corrects yellow/grey age tint into crisp eye-catching bright white.

Suitable For:

  • 5-Star Hotels & Resorts: Guest linen, kitchen rotisseries, and banquet tableware
  • Hospitals & Healthcare: Medical ward linen, surgical scrubs, and sanitized surfaces
  • Commercial Laundries: High-capacity washer-extractors and continuous batch tunnels
  • Industrial Kitchens: Heavy grease hood baffles, dishwashers, and food-prep areas

🛡️ Safety & Handling Guidelines

Premix with water before adding to machine to prevent localized fabric spotting.

Storage & Transport:

  • Store in original UN-approved heavy-duty HDPE jerrican containers.
  • Keep in a cool, well-ventilated dry warehouse away from direct sunlight.
  • Keep container tightly sealed when not in active commercial dosing.
  • Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS / SDS) available on technical request.

Frequently Asked Questions About NEEL

What is the standard commercial dilution ratio for NEEL?

NEEL is formulated for commercial efficiency. Standard dosage is: 0.2 to 0.5 grams per kg dry linen dissolved in water during final rinse.. Adjust concentration based on soil intensity and water hardness.

What commercial institutions commonly use NEEL?

NEEL is widely supplied to 5-star hotels, tertiary hospitals, commercial laundries, and institutional facilities across Rawalpindi, Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, and nationwide.

What packaging sizes are available for NEEL?

Standard commercial supply is in 25 Kg sealed HDPE jerricans. Optional packaging includes 5 Kg cans, 10 Kg containers, and 200 Kg bulk drums for industrial laundries and washhouses.
