Technical Infrastructure & Capabilities

Engineered Chemical Systems & Physical Infrastructure

Advanced chemical synthesis, precision wash-program engineering, and heavy-duty bulk packaging lines on our Rawalpindi manufacturing campus.

01 Analytical Quality Control

QC & R&D laboratories

Every batch is checked for active concentration, pH, viscosity, and stability. R&D continues to develop caustic-free biological formulas and cold-water detergents.

  • Active concentration verification: Titration and refractive index checks on every production run.
  • pH & viscosity benchmarking: Tight tolerances ensure consistent dosing through automated pumps.
  • Caustic-free bio formulas: Enzyme-based laundry solutions that reduce fabric degradation.
  • Cold-water detergency: Active surfactant chemistry engineered for ambient temperature cleaning.
100% Batch Tested · Zero-Defect QA Protocol
02 Textile & Thermal Engineering

Wash-program technology

Formulations are designed for 40°C to 90°C cycles — protecting linen fibre life, lifting whiteness, and lowering hotel laundry energy costs.

  • 40°C to 90°C thermal cycles: Multi-temperature adaptability for delicate linens through heavy kitchen terry.
  • Linen fibre life protection: Controlled alkalinity and anti-chlor souring extend textile replacement cycles.
  • Superior whiteness lift: Advanced optical brighteners and oxygen-bleach synergy restore brilliant white.
  • Laundry energy reduction: Lower cycle temperatures cut institutional boiler and steam fuel expenses.
35%+ Extended Linen Life · Substantial Energy Savings
03 Industrial Packaging Fleet
