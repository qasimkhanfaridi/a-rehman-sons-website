Company Profile

Quality, Value, Service

Founded in 1988, A. Rehman & Sons manufactures high-performance commercial detergents in a purpose-built factory with in-house QC and R&D laboratories in Rawalpindi.

Manufacturing & Innovation

  • Purpose-built manufacturing facility, Rawalpindi
  • In-house Quality Control Laboratory
  • Research & Development laboratory
  • Biological products formulated without caustic
  • Liquid feed systems — pioneered 35 years ago
  • Water flush delivery system for liquid detergents
